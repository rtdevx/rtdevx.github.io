`Artificial intelligence` is the capability of computational systems to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making... blah blah blah...

- `Perceptive AI` - Systems that can see, hear and understand.
- `Predictive AI` - Systems that can learn from data to make decisions.
- `Generative AI` - Systems that can create new content like text, images or audio. Gen AI systems can also communicate.

- `NLP` - Natural Language Processing
- `ASR` - Automatic Speech Recognition
- `ML` - Machine Learning
